About PADECO NEWS

Founded by Bush Smith, PADECO NEWS is an independent platform dedicated to investigative journalism, political accountability, and cultural reporting. Every story is fact-checked and independent — no sponsors, no hidden ties.

Our History

PADECO NEWS was founded in 2018 by Bush Smith. Starting as a small newsletter, it grew into an independent journalism platform covering politics, technology, culture, and investigations. By 2026, it reaches over 150,000 monthly readers — always independent and reader-supported.
